2. Principle: no data collection

Compasswork does not process any personal data on the provider's servers. The app works entirely offline. There are no user accounts, no registration, no analytics or tracking services, no advertising and no sharing of data with third parties.

3. Data stored on the device

Everything you create in the app stays exclusively on your device. The following is stored:

  • your sheets: name, drawings (lines, arcs, freehand strokes, points and texts), the last view, the position of the drawing instruments, a small preview image and, where applicable, a task that has been set (congruence condition and given parts)
  • your settings, such as language, appearance, paper type, colour and line width, toolbar position, pencil behaviour and any screen size calibration
  • whether you have already drawn with a stylus, so that fingers only move things afterwards and do not draw by accident

Sheets are stored using the browser technology IndexedDB and settings using localStorage, both in the protected app directory. They are never transmitted. Uninstalling the app removes them completely. Individual sheets can be deleted in the app under “My sheets”.

Texts you write on a sheet are, like all other content, stored locally only. Please do not write names or other personal details of students on sheets you intend to share.

4. Export, import, PDF and QR codes

Exporting creates a file (extension .zirkelwerk) and passes it to your device's share function. Where the file goes from there (e.g. the Files app, AirDrop or email) is entirely up to you. When importing, you choose a file yourself; the app reads only that file.

A PDF of a sheet is likewise created on the device only and passed to the share function (e.g. for printing).

A task QR code contains only the task itself: for lab tasks the congruence condition and the given parts, for a shared sheet its name and drawing. It is created in the app and shown on screen. Compasswork does not access the camera for this: the device's camera app scans the code and hands the link to Compasswork. The app checks the content and creates a sheet on the device from it; nothing is transmitted.

5. Permissions

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts or location. The device's share function may offer to save an image to the photo library; the system may ask for your permission to do so. The app neither reads nor changes existing photos.

6. Internet connections

The app does not establish any network connections on its own. It contains no external libraries, no fonts from third-party servers and no embedded content.

7. In-app purchase

If you purchase the full version, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. The provider of this app does not receive any payment details. The app only learns from the store whether the full version is unlocked for your account and remembers this on the device so that it also works offline. Apple's or Google's privacy policies apply.

8. Distribution via an app store

When you download the app from an app store, its operator processes data on its own responsibility (such as account, device and time of download). The provider of this app has no influence on this. The privacy policy of the respective operator applies.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As the provider does not store any personal data, requests for access or erasure addressed to the provider will generally have no effect. You can delete all data yourself in the app at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated whenever app features relevant to data protection change. The current version is available in the app and on this page.
